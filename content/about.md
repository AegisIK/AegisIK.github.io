I'm a PhD student at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/) advised by
[Amir Barati Farimani](https://sites.google.com/view/barati). My research uses machine
learning for materials and drug discovery, automated scientific discovery, and
large-scale atomistic simulation.

Previously, I worked on LLMs at **JPMorgan Chase's** Commercial Bank AI team and at
**Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory**. I graduated from **UC Berkeley** with a
Bachelor's in Computer Science, where I was fortunate to work with
[Gerbrand Ceder](https://ceder.berkeley.edu/) and
[Bowen Deng](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bowen-deng-16390b264) on
[CHGNet](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-023-00716-3) and distributed inference
for machine learning interatomic potentials.

I come primarily from a machine learning background rather than a chemistry or biology
one. My work spans PyTorch, Cython, performance programming in C, and CUDA. I also enjoy
full-stack engineering, and have built large-scale systems for startups and for the
[UC Berkeley EECS Department](https://eecs.berkeley.edu/).

If you have questions about Berkeley, CMU, finding internships or research, or general
life stuff, feel free to email me at [kevinhan@cmu.edu](mailto:kevinhan@cmu.edu).
